\section{Discussion and conclusion}\label{sec:discussion}

The central finding is that the UK tax--benefit system responds differently
when a common aggregate earnings loss is distributed differently across
workers and labour-market states. Across the main-table cells---both loss
anchors at the duration-equivalent scales that survive the support
screen---the wage-cut and displacement bounds differ by
\SubmissionCushionDifferenceMin--\SubmissionCushionDifferenceMax\ percentage
points, though the top of that range comes from the three- and six-month
cells, which are exactly the ones the annualisation bounding of
Appendix~\ref{app:monthlyuc} shows to be conservative by about
\MonthlyUCCushionGap\ points; at the unit twelve-month scale the difference is
\PrimaryCushionDifference\ points and the wage-cut Exchequer cost is
\pounds\PrimaryExchequerDifference\ million higher. (The excluded OBR
three-month cell, whose displacement draw rests on
\OBRThreeMonthEffectiveLossRecords\ effective record contributions, gives
\SubmissionThinCellCushionDifference\ points and is reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:mc} as sensitivity evidence only, and is excluded from the
headline range for the same support reason that excludes it from the table.) The factorial middle cell splits that difference into
\FactorialConcStep\ points from concentrating the loss on selected workers
and only \FactorialStateStep\ points from the employment-state change
itself; the record-resampling exercise places the total contrast at
\BootstrapCushionDifference\ points (sensitivity range
\BootstrapCushionDifferenceCILower--\BootstrapCushionDifferenceCIUpper), and
it survives the removal of any single loss-contributing record
(\LOOMin--\LOOMax\ points). The mechanism is a loss-concentration effect
inside the statutory schedule: diffuse cuts are relieved at marginal
income-tax rates while complete individual losses exhaust that relief and
land on means-tested UC.

The near-zero employment-state step carries much weaker information than the
concentration step, and the paper does not rest on it. Every
status-contingent institution---New Style JSA, monthly assessment periods,
the five-week wait, claiming frictions, conditionality---sits outside the
annual model, so the two cells being compared differ only by a label no
modelled formula reads. New Style JSA alone would be worth up to
\JSACushionPoints\ percentage points of cushioning for a representative
displaced worker, tens of times the step actually measured. The
decomposition therefore bounds what a static statutory model can attribute
to the extensive margin; it is not evidence that job loss and an equivalent
in-work earnings collapse are fiscally alike in the United Kingdom.

On the UC side four objects must
be kept distinct: statutory entitlement as represented by PolicyEngine;
entitlement conditional on the incomplete household information in the FRS
(no usable liquid-capital measure, so the capital gates are not exercised);
modelled take-up, which is a scenario convention; and actual receipt
following real job loss, which none of the above observes. The simulation
evidence speaks to the first three. Two sensitivity results sharpen this
reading in opposite directions. Under a pension-gross income concept the
cushioning \emph{levels} fall by roughly four percentage points on both
margins while the contrast widens slightly, from \GapHBAIPension\ to
\GapPensionGross\ points, so the gap is a schedule effect rather than an
artefact of the HBAI treatment of pension saving---and this is one of the
sensitivities under which it does not shrink. The
claiming convention, by contrast, is not settled and the paper should not
pretend otherwise. The new-entitlement take-up grid returns numerically
identical results under every rate and under the stale flag---identical to
machine precision, which is only possible if its re-draw set is empty, a
conclusion \TakeupInertBasis---so it tests an empty set rather than
establishing insensitivity. The convention that does bind is the frozen
claiming state of already-entitled non-claimers. On \TakeupEntitledSource,
the only source at which either quantity has been measured, that
convention zeroes UC for \TakeupZeroUCNonTakeupShare\ per cent of the
displaced by weight, and re-drawing it moves displacement cushioning by
\TakeupEntitledSpread\ percentage points. A
spread of that order is comparable to the headline gap itself. The contrast
is pre-specified and conditional: it prices a statutory schedule effect, not
the tariffs' total causal incidence, and its magnitude is conditional on a
claiming rule the survey data cannot pin down. The annualisation of
unemployment spells is conservative for it: the parameter-based bounding in
Appendix~\ref{app:monthlyuc} shows that correct monthly UC and partial-year
PAYE accounting would raise displacement cushioning at sub-annual durations
and leave the 12-month contrast exact.

Balanced integration makes the common aggregate loss comparable assignment
by assignment and closely reproduces the Bernoulli mean at unit scale. It
does not overcome sparse information: the unit displacement loss has only
\UnitEffectiveLossRecords\ effective record contributions, and support is
weaker at the smallest OBR-duration cells. Assignment SDs describe
scenario-allocation sensitivity; record-composition sensitivity is scored
separately by the household resampling exercise and the leave-one-record-out
audit, neither of which is a design-based confidence interval, and no
significance claim is attached to any interval. The sign also survives all
\LeaveSectorCount\ leave-one-division-out reruns at \LeaveSectorDraws\ assignments,
with a cushioning difference of \LeaveSectorCushionDifferenceMin--%
\LeaveSectorCushionDifferenceMax\ points. This establishes sector robustness
within the imposed bridge, not causal robustness of that bridge.

Calibrated longitudinal-LFS allocation sensitivities preserve the sign of the
contrast but not its magnitude. Displacement cushioning spans
\LFSSelectionCushionMin--\LFSSelectionCushionMax\ per cent across cell,
earnings-band and regularised-QRF risk shapes, against
\LFSUniformCushion\ per cent under the uniform assignment used elsewhere in
the paper. Every LFS-shaped model raises cushioning
(\LFSSelectionShiftMin--\LFSSelectionShiftMax\ points) and so narrows the
headline gap to \LFSSelectionGapMin--\LFSSelectionGapMax\ points---a
narrowing of a fifth to a third of the contrast. This exercise locates a
first-order sensitivity rather than establishing robustness. One accounting
point travels with it: the selection runs and their uniform benchmark use
the 100-draw Bernoulli design, whose own wage-minus-displacement gap is
\LFSSelectionBaselineGap\ points, not the 50-draw balanced
\PrimaryCushionDifference\ of the primary table; the narrowing is measured
within the former. The models agree closely with each other
but weakly person by person, and the available LFS manufacturing target has
only \LFSManufacturingDonors\ donors, so stability of an aggregate fiscal
ratio across three imputations is not evidence that tariff-specific worker
selection has been identified.

National poverty changes remain near zero because the exposed population is
small and often above the poverty line. They are not precise population
estimates and do not imply that affected households' losses are small. EPD,
inactivity, reallocation and demographic results are secondary or
supplementary rather than competing headline estimands.

The common-loss transition-equivalent path makes the interior of the surface
concrete: some workers receive an annualized re-employment penalty, some
re-enter services at lower earnings, and survivors absorb the residual loss.
Under the illustrative 70/30 transition allocation, the simulated
cushioning is \FullTransitionCushion\ per cent and the Exchequer cost is
\pounds\FullTransitionExchequer\ million. These are conditional results: the
annualized lag is encoded in earnings and hours rather than a monthly
unemployment-benefit spell; the transition share, three-month lag and 28.3 per
cent services penalty are
scenario inputs, and the latter is a cross-sectional FRS destination gap that
also captures hours and part-time composition. The response surface, including
50/50 and 85/15 allocations and the 14 per cent penalty, is therefore more
informative than any single preferred point.

The principal limitation is that the analysis begins after the production-side
transmission mechanism. It does not estimate how tariffs affect prices,
quantities, output, value added, imported inputs, productivity, investment,
profits, or labour demand. Instead, it maps a declared sector earnings loss
directly onto households and evaluates the statutory response. The resulting
estimates therefore identify fiscal incidence conditional on the imposed loss
and adjustment path. They do not identify the economy-wide effect of the trade
policy. Worker selection is independent within broad SIC divisions; the
longitudinal LFS models provide only weakly concordant predictive rankings;
within-year benefit timing, New Style JSA, household labour supply, indirect
taxes, exchange rates, retaliation, and general equilibrium remain outside the
central specification. The shock reaches employees only, so self-employed
workers in the exposed divisions have no counterpart margin here.
Constituency machinery in the Online Supplement is
illustrative because local industry structure is not observed at the required
level.

Five items of unfinished business should be named rather than left for a
reader to discover. The \texttt{all\_entitled} take-up grid, which is the
specification in which the claiming assumption actually binds, has been
implemented but not yet run at the unit-stress calibration; the manuscript
quotes a superseded-vintage bound for it. The product--destination panel has
not been regenerated under the anticipation-window, continuous-trade or
Poisson specifications now implemented in its estimator, so the reported
trade estimates retain an anticipation bias of known sign and a zero-fill
bias of unknown sign, and its figure retains a base period inside the
anticipation window. The channel decomposition still runs
on \FactorialChannelSeeds\ seeds against a \FactorialDraws-draw headline, and
should be re-run at the full count so the two reconcile. The per-record
balanced-versus-Bernoulli inclusion-probability diagnostic
(\path{analysis/assignment_inclusion_diagnostic.py}), which would show
whether the highest-weight exposed records are selected at their nominal
rates, is implemented but unrun. And the household bootstrap holds
the original assignment fixed, so its interval is conditional on which records
those draws selected and is narrower than a fully re-drawn bootstrap would be.
None of the five is \emph{known} to change the sign of the reported
contrast, which is a weaker statement than that none of them would. The
claiming grid alone moved displacement cushioning by
\TakeupEntitledSpread\ points at the calibration where it was last run,
against a \PrimaryCushionDifference-point gap; adding the worker-selection
shift and the omitted New Style JSA, all three pointing the same way, would
leave a contrast of a few points rather than ten. The sign is likely to
survive and the magnitude is not. The second item bears only on the trade
benchmark rather than on the cushioning estimand.

The framework nevertheless provides a useful platform for subsequent work.
The next empirical step is to combine product- or firm-level evidence on
tariff exposure, prices, quantities, output, and worker outcomes with the
household microsimulation. Such evidence would replace the imposed earnings
bridge with an estimated transmission mechanism while retaining the adjustment
margin and fiscal-incidence decomposition developed here.

The main conclusion is that, conditional on a common earnings loss, the
labour-market margin materially changes the distribution of tax relief, benefit
support, poverty effects, and public-finance costs. Wage-cut and displacement
specifications are transparent bounds; the mixed and transition-equivalent
paths show how intermediate adjustment patterns alter those outcomes. The
results therefore speak directly to the fiscal consequences of labour-market
adjustment, while leaving the size and composition of the underlying trade
shock as an empirical question for future research.
