\noindent This online supplement collects the exploratory and benchmark
material referenced from the main paper: the elasticity/pass-through grid,
the reallocation sensitivity, the observed-outturn stress scenario, the HMRC
destination-panel diagnostic, the Economic Prosperity Deal counterfactual
(withdrawn as a quantitative result), the demographic diagnostic, the
supply-chain sensitivity, and the constituency machinery. All numerical
values are generated from the pinned artifacts in the replication package;
conventions (assignment SDs after $\pm$, exploratory draw counts) follow the
main paper's Monte Carlo appendix.
