\section{First-pass results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{The comparative anatomy}\label{sec:results-anatomy}

Table~\ref{tab:ms-anatomy} is the paper's central object: the five
episodes side by side under the common lens. Three regularities
organise everything that follows. First, \emph{Britain's negative trade
shocks were regressive and its positive ones were not progressive}: the
energy shock cost the bottom decile \MsEnergyGrossPctSpendDecOne\ per
cent of its spending against \MsEnergyGrossPctSpendDecTen\ per cent at
the top, the TCA food shock \MsTcaPctSpendDecOne\ against
\MsTcaPctSpendDecTen\ per cent, while the India agreement's consumer
gains are spread almost exactly proportionally
(\MsCetaBottomPctSpend\ versus \MsCetaTopPctSpend\ per cent of
spending) and are two orders of magnitude smaller. Second,
\emph{intensity per pound of shock differs}: normalised per
\pounds 1 billion of gross shock, the energy episode put
\MsEnergyNormBottomPctPerBn\ points of bottom-decile spending at
stake, the TCA \MsTcaNormBottomPctPerBn, and the CETA gains only
\MsCetaNormBottomPctPerBn---the composition of a trade shock, not just
its size, determines who bears it. Third, \emph{the cushioning came
from different parts of the state}: the one earnings-led episode was
cushioned automatically (\MsUSTariffDisplacementCushionPct--%
\MsUSTariffWageCutCushionPct\ per cent through the tax--benefit
system, from the companion study), while the price-led energy episode
was cushioned by discretion---the Energy Price Guarantee absorbed
\MsEnergyCushionSharePct\ per cent of the gross shock---and the
automatic price-side mechanism, benefit indexation, arrived a year
late (Section~\ref{sec:results-rulebook}).

\subsection{Episode detail}

\paragraph{TCA food prices.} An 8 per cent NTB-attributed food price
rise costs households \pounds\MsTcaAnnGbpDecOne\ (bottom decile) to
\pounds\MsTcaAnnGbpDecTen\ (top decile) a year at end-state---
regressive as a share of spending (\MsTcaPctSpendDecOne\ versus
\MsTcaPctSpendDecTen\ per cent) because food budget shares fall with
income. The pipeline's cumulative aggregate cross-check against the
published \pounds\MsTcaPublishedCumulGbp\ per household comes in at
ratio \MsTcaCrossCheckRatio\ under a linear phase-in: the published
estimate implies the price effect was back-loaded (an average effect
about \MsTcaImpliedAvgEffectShare\ of end-state over the window),
which the first-pass linear ramp overstates. The diagnostic is
reported, not calibrated away; the microsimulation stage will carry
the published path.

\paragraph{The energy shock and the EPG.} At cap levels, the gross
price shock (\pounds\MsEnergyCapBase\ to \pounds\MsEnergyCapPeak\
typical bill, +\MsEnergyGrossFactorPct\ per cent) would have cost the
bottom decile \pounds\MsEnergyGrossGbpDecOne\ a year---%
\MsEnergyGrossPctSpendDecOne\ per cent of its total spending, more
than double the top decile's share. The Energy Price Guarantee
truncated the rise to +\MsEnergyNetFactorPct\ per cent, absorbing
\pounds\MsEnergyCushionGbpDecOne\ a year for a bottom-decile household
and \MsEnergyCushionSharePct\ per cent of the aggregate gross shock
(\pounds\MsEnergyCushionAggBn\ billion a year at the cap-level
run-rate; \pounds\MsEnergyCushionAggSixMonthsBn\ billion over the
six-month EPG window, the same order as the OBR's
\pounds\MsEnergyObrEpgBn\ billion costing). Because the EPG capped the
\emph{price}, its cushioning share is constant across deciles: a
proportional subsidy on a regressive shock, so the post-EPG burden
remained regressive---a design fact the household-record stage can
contrast with targeted alternatives.

\paragraph{The US tariffs.} The UK consumer side is zero---no
retaliation occurred---so this episode's incidence is entirely
earnings-led: a gross loss of \pounds\MsUSTariffGrossShockMillions\
million a year cushioned by the automatic tax--benefit system at
\MsUSTariffDisplacementCushionPct\ per cent (displacement) to
\MsUSTariffWageCutCushionPct\ per cent (wage cuts), the companion
study's headline carried over as this episode's second stage.

\paragraph{The India gains, and the placebo.} At full pass-through the
CETA duty cuts are worth \pounds\MsCetaGainGbpPtHundredDecOne\ a year
to a bottom-decile household and \pounds\MsCetaGainGbpPtHundredDecTen\
to a top-decile one---\MsCetaVerdict\ as a share of spending, and
economically negligible at any pass-through dial
(\pounds\MsCetaGainGbpPtFiftyDecOne--\MsCetaGainGbpPtFiftyDecTen\ at
50 per cent). The CPTPP placebo behaves as a placebo should: its
\pounds\MsCptppGdpBn\ billion long-run GDP estimate divides to
\pounds\MsCptppNaiveGbpPerHousehold\ per household per year with no
defensible distributional structure, and the pipeline reports exactly
that and nothing more.

\subsection{The rulebook seed results}\label{sec:results-rulebook}

The vintage axis is seeded with two statutory facts. First, the
uprating lag: Universal Credit's standard allowance rose 3.1 per cent
in April 2022 (indexed to the previous September's inflation) while
prices rose at double digits, so a single adult on UC fell
\pounds\MsUcLagCostGbp\ behind contemporaneous indexation over
2022--23---\MsUcLagCostPctAllowance\ per cent of the annual allowance,
lost to timing alone, at exactly the moment the terms-of-trade shock
peaked. Second, sequencing: the \pounds\MsUcUpliftRemovalGbp-a-year
Covid uplift was removed in October 2021, months before the energy
shock landed, so the lowest-income households entered the largest
price-led trade shock of the period from a rulebook trough. Together
with the EPG's 46-per-cent discretionary absorption, the seed picture
of the British response to the 2020s trade shocks is: \emph{automatic
on the earnings margin, discretionary on the price margin, and lagged
precisely where the price shocks bit hardest}. The microsimulation
stage turns these representative-household facts into
population-weighted decompositions.
